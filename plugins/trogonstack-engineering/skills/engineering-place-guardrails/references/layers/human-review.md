# Layer 5: Human Review

A person notices and decides. The scarcest resource, the slowest feedback, and the least consistent result. Paid on every pull request.

## What Belongs Here

- Is this the right problem, and the right design for it.
- Is the trade-off acceptable for users and the business.
- Does the change fit where the system is going.

## What Does Not

Any mechanical catch: style, naming, banned calls, missing tests, wrong layer dependencies. Each time a reviewer makes one, a guardrail is missing higher up. Turn it into one and remove it from the checklist.

## Signal To Collect

Recurring review comments are the cheapest source of promotion candidates. Group them by mistake, count recurrences, and walk each group down the pyramid from the top.
